**Authors:** Junteng Liu, Shiqi Chen, Yu Cheng, Junxian He

**Year:** 2024

**Venue:** EMNLP 2024

**First author:** Junteng Liu

This paper investigates the existence of a universal truthfulness hyperplane inside large language models. The authors demonstrate that there exists a linear direction in the model's representation space that can distinguish between truthful and untruthful statements across different tasks and domains. This finding provides valuable insights into LLM interpretability and offers a promising direction for improving model truthfulness.
