**Authors:** Jinghan Zhang, Shiqi Chen, Junteng Liu, Junxian He

**Year:** 2023

**Venue:** NeurIPS 2023

**Co-author:** Junteng Liu

This paper explores the use of arithmetic operations for composing parameter-efficient modules. The authors demonstrate that parameter-efficient modules (such as adapters and LoRA modules) can be effectively composed using simple arithmetic operations, enabling efficient model adaptation and composition without full fine-tuning.
