**Authors:** Junteng Liu, Weihao Zeng, Xiwen Zhang, Yijun Wang, Zifei Shan, Junxian He

**Year:** 2025

**Venue:** Arxiv

**First author:** Junteng Liu

This paper investigates the perception bottleneck of Vision-Language Models (VLMs) when applied to chart understanding tasks. Through extensive analysis, the authors identify key limitations in how VLMs process and interpret visual information from charts, and propose strategies to mitigate these bottlenecks for improved visual reasoning performance.
