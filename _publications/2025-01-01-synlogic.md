**Authors:** Junteng Liu, Yuanxiang Fan, Zhuo Jiang, Han Ding, Yongyi Hu, Chi Zhang, Yiqi Shi, Shitong Weng, Aili Chen, Shiqi Chen, Yunan Huang, Mozhi Zhang, Pengyu Zhao, Junjie Yan, Junxian He

**Year:** 2025

**Venue:** Arxiv

**First author:** Junteng Liu

This paper introduces SynLogic, a novel framework for synthesizing high-quality, verifiable reasoning data at scale. The framework enables the generation of diverse logical reasoning examples with automatically verifiable answers, significantly reducing the need for manual annotation and improving the logical reasoning capabilities of large language models.
