**Authors:** Shiqi Chen, Miao Xiong, Junteng Liu, Zhengxuan Wu, Teng Xiao, Siyang Gao, Junxian He

**Year:** 2024

**Venue:** ICML 2024

**Co-author:** Junteng Liu

This paper explores the use of in-context sharpness from an inner representation perspective for hallucination mitigation in large language models. The authors demonstrate that the sharpness of in-context representations can serve as an effective alert mechanism for detecting and mitigating hallucinations in model outputs.
